# Abstain or Escalate

## Inputs
- Evidence quality, unresolved objections, missing inputs, and decision impact.

## Use when
- Evidence is incomplete, contradictory, stale, or outside the configured authority.

## Steps
1. Identify the blocking uncertainty or policy boundary.
2. Decide whether a bounded provisional result is safe.
3. Otherwise specify the human decision or missing evidence required.
4. Preserve limitations and do not convert unknowns into confidence.

## Output
- Proceed, provisional, abstain, or escalate decision with reasons.

## Abstention
- Abstain from a consequential recommendation when acceptance conditions are unmet.
