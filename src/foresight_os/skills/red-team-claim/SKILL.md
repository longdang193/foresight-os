# Red-Team Claim

## Inputs
- A claim or recommendation, supporting evidence, assumptions, and counterevidence.

## Use when
- A consequential statement needs adversarial challenge before acceptance.

## Steps
1. Restate the strongest version of the target claim.
2. Search for contradictory evidence and alternative explanations.
3. Identify hidden assumptions and failure conditions.
4. Disposition each objection as accepted, rejected, or unresolved.

## Output
- Objections, evidence identifiers, dispositions, and unresolved issues.

## Abstention
- Abstain when the target or evidence boundary is not identifiable.
