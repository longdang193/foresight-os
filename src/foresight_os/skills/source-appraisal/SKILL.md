# Source Appraisal

## Inputs
- A source, excerpt, locator, version, date, and retrieval limitations.

## Use when
- A claim depends on external or retrieved source material.

## Steps
1. Identify the source and the exact proposition it supports.
2. Check recency, authority, provenance, scope, and methodological limits.
3. Separate observed content from interpretation.
4. Record missing context and what would disconfirm the appraisal.

## Output
- Source identity, quality assessment, support boundary, and limitations.

## Abstention
- Abstain when provenance or the relevant excerpt cannot be verified.
